How to read this page

Produced by is the validator whose signature seals the block. Validators take turns in a fixed rotation read from the staking contract.

In turn means the block came from the validator whose turn it was. Backup means that validator missed its slot and the next one stepped in after 10 seconds.

Gap is the time since the previous block. The target is 2 seconds.
